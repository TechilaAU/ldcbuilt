01 Overview

Six layouts from 214m² to 359m², the widest bedroom spread in the collection at three to five. Every variant sits on the same 16.7m frontage, so you can move up or down the range without changing the block. The two largest add a fifth bedroom and a powder room.

Enquire on the Harrington All designs
03 Standard inclusions
  • Fixed-price QBCC contract with a detailed inclusions schedule
  • Engineered slab and frame to site classification
  • Colorbond roofing with 450mm eaves
  • 2400mm ceilings with 2100mm door and window heights
  • Stone benchtops to kitchen and wet areas
  • Ducted air conditioning provision
  • Livable Housing Design Standard 2022 compliance
  • Site supervision from a single point of contact
