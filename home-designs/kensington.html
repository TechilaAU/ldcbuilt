01 Overview

Five single-storey layouts from 212m² to 283m², all four bedroom bar one, built around a central living, dining and kitchen zone with the master and ensuite set apart from the secondary bedrooms. Every layout carries a double garage, alfresco and a separate retreat or activity space. Needs a wider block than most single-storey designs.

  • Fixed-price QBCC contract with a detailed inclusions schedule
